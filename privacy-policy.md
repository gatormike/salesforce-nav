# Privacy Policy — SetupPilot for Salesforce

**Last updated: 2026-07-14**

## Summary

SetupPilot for Salesforce does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What data is stored

The extension stores the following data **locally in your browser only**, using the Chrome `storage.local` API:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Your navigation menu configuration (page labels and Salesforce Setup paths) | So your custom menu persists across browser sessions |
| Recently visited Salesforce Setup page paths and labels | Powers the history dropdown |

## What data is NOT collected

- No data is sent to any external server
- No analytics or telemetry
- No personal information, usernames, or account details
- No Salesforce record data

## Third-party services

None. The extension makes no network requests.

## Changes to this policy

Any future changes will be reflected here with an updated date and noted in the repository changelog.
